# Account Support

Customers can create an account using an email address and password.

If a customer forgets their password, they can reset it using the password reset link on the login page.

Customers can update their email address, shipping address, and phone number in the account settings page.

For security reasons, customer support cannot see or share a customer's password.

Customers can request account deletion by contacting customer support.

Account deletion may take up to 14 business days to complete.
