# Return Policy

Customers can return most products within 30 days of delivery.

Returned items must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.

Refunds are usually processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Final sale items, gift cards, and customized products cannot be returned.

If a product arrives damaged or defective, customers should contact customer support within 7 days of delivery and provide photos of the damaged item.

The company may reject returns that do not meet the return policy conditions.
